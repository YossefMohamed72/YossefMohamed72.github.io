Delica

A named kabocha-type hybrid, with a flattened shape and thick yellow flesh.

Takii identifies Delica F1 as a dark-green, flat-globe squash with thick yellow flesh and a sweet flavour. Delica is a specific commercial hybrid, not a label for every green kabocha.

Culinary applications

Suitable for cooked squash preparations. Confirm texture and culinary performance with a sample from the offered lot.

Storage & handling

Keep mature, sound fruit ventilated and protect it from bruising. General winter-squash guidance is 12.5–15°C and 50–70% relative humidity; green-rind types need cultivar-specific handling. Shelf life depends on cultivar, maturity and handling.
