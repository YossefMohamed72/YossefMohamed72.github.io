Butternut

The familiar elongated squash with smooth tan skin and sweet orange flesh.

Butternut is a squash type encompassing multiple cultivars. Elsoms describes the characteristic elongated shape, smooth tan skin and sweet orange flesh. Exact size, grade and cultivar should be agreed for each order.

Culinary applications

A versatile choice for cooked dishes; specify your roasting, soup or processing requirements.

Storage & handling

Keep mature, sound fruit ventilated and protect it from bruising. General winter-squash guidance is 12.5–15°C and 50–70% relative humidity; green-rind types need cultivar-specific handling. Shelf life depends on cultivar, maturity and handling.
