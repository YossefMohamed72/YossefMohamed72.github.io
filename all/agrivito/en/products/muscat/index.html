A catalogue selection requiring confirmation of its full cultivar name.

Muscat is retained as the requested commercial name. Musquée de Provence is a documented pumpkin cultivar, but its correspondence to AgriVito’s Muscat requires confirmation. Specifications will depend on the confirmed selection.

Storage & handling

Keep mature, sound fruit ventilated and protect it from bruising. General winter-squash guidance is 12.5–15°C and 50–70% relative humidity; green-rind types need cultivar-specific handling. Shelf life depends on cultivar, maturity and handling.
