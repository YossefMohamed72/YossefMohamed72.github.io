01 / Squash & pumpkins

Protect the rind. Respect maturity.

Assess maturity, shape, stem condition and handling damage. Check the expected internal colour against the cultivar.

Keep mature, sound fruit ventilated and protect it from bruising. General winter-squash guidance is 12.5–15°C and 50–70% relative humidity; green-rind types need cultivar-specific handling. Shelf life depends on cultivar, maturity and handling.

02 / Artichokes

Compact buds. Careful cooling.

Evaluate head compactness, bract condition and damage. Make the size grade explicit in the commercial specification.

Fresh globe artichokes need rapid cooling and gentle handling. General guidance is 0°C and above 95% relative humidity; storage potential is generally under 21 days. Assess remaining shelf life for the actual lot.
