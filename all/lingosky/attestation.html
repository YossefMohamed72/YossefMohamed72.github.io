FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does document attestation take? +

Processing time depends on the document, issuing country, embassy or consulate requirements and the UAE authority involved. Contact us for an assessment of your specific case.

Yes. We can coordinate the applicable embassy or consulate stage depending on the country and document type.
